Questions

Good to know

Does ClearDesk move or copy my files?

Neither. It stores a link to where each file already lives. Removing an item only takes it off ClearDesk. The file stays in its folder.

What happens if I rename or move a file?

ClearDesk notices the next time you open the panel. If it can find the file nearby, it shows “Moved” and relinks with one click. If not, it says “Missing” and lets you find it again or remove it.

Does it search my whole computer?

No, on purpose. It searches only what you added to workspaces, which is why it’s instant and private. For everything else, your system search is great.

Will the shortcut clash with Spotlight or my keyboard language?

No. The default is Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘⇧Space on Mac). ClearDesk refuses Ctrl+Space and ⌘Space, and you can pick any shortcut you like.

How does launch pricing work?

The first 5 licences sell at the Founding price and the next 20 at the Early price. After that it’s the regular price. The price at checkout is always the tier that’s live at that moment.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Download it and use one workspace with up to 50 items, free forever. A licence unlocks unlimited workspaces and items. Paste the key in Settings and you’re done.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pay once and keep it. Every 1.x update is free.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, for any reason. Reply to your receipt email.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (Mica effects on 11), and macOS 11 Big Sur or newer on Apple silicon and Intel.
